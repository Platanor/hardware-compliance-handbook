**RED key dates and deadlines: the directive, the cybersecurity delegated act, and the standards**

RED's timeline has three separate layers that are easy to conflate: the directive's own entry-into-force and application dates (settled since 2016), the cybersecurity-specific delegated act's application date (settled since 2023, effective 2025), and the harmonised-standards publication (settled since January 2025, but with restrictions — see [`essential-requirements.md`](essential-requirements.md)).

**Layer 1 — the directive itself**

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22 May 2014 | Published in the Official Journal (OJ L 153) | Directive 2014/53/EU |
| 21 June 2014 | Entry into force (20th day after publication) | Art. 51 |
| 12 June 2016 | Deadline for Member States to adopt and publish transposing national laws | Art. 49(1) |
| **13 June 2016** | RED becomes applicable — Member States apply their transposing measures from this date; Directive 1999/5/EC repealed | Art. 49(1), Art. 50 |
| 13 June 2017 | End of the transitional window during which equipment compliant with the pre-RED regime, placed on the market before this date, may still be made available/put into service | Art. 48 |

**Layer 2 — the cybersecurity delegated act (Article 3(3)(d)(e)(f))**

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 August 2022 | Commission Implementing Decision C(2022) 5637 — standardisation request to CEN/Cenelec for harmonised standards supporting Art. 3(3)(d)(e)(f) | Referenced in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2444, recital 3 |
| 12 January 2022 | Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 published in the OJ (adopted 29 October 2021) | (EU) 2022/30 |

| 27 October 2023 | Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2444 published — moves the application date and corrects an error in Art. 1(2) of (EU) 2022/30 | (EU) 2023/2444 |
| **1 August 2025** | **Current, binding application date.** Art. 3(3)(d)(e)(f) become mandatory essential requirements for the product categories named in (EU) 2022/30 | (EU) 2023/2444, Art. 1 |

The push from 2024 to 2025 was explicitly to give CEN and Cenelec more time — the Commission's own recitals describe the underlying standardisation work (cybersecurity: network, personal data/privacy, and fraud protection) as complex and unprecedented for this category of harmonised standard.

**Putting the three layers together: what applies when, in practice**

- **Before 1 August 2025:** RED's unconditional requirements (Art. 3(1)–(2): health/safety, EMC, spectrum efficiency) already applied to any radio equipment placed on the market since 13 June 2016. The cybersecurity-specific points (d)(e)(f) did not yet bind anyone — no delegated act had taken effect.
- **From 1 August 2025 onward:** for internet-connected, childcare, toy, or wearable radio equipment falling within the categories named in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30, points (d), (e), and/or (f) are binding essential requirements. A conformity assessment covering these points is required before the equipment can lawfully carry the CE mark and be placed on the market — this is not a future obligation, it is current law.
- **From 30 January 2025 onward (technically ahead of the 1 August 2025 application date):** the EN 18031 series is available for manufacturers who want to work toward self-declared presumption of conformity — but only for the parts of each standard the Commission accepted without restriction.

**No transitional grace period for RED's cybersecurity requirements.** Unlike the CRA, which grandfathers products already on the market before its full-application date (subject to conditions — see [`timeline-deadlines.md`](../cra/timeline-deadlines.md)), RED's Article 3(3)(d)(e)(f) apply from 1 August 2025 to any in-scope equipment being placed on the market from that date forward. There is no equivalent CRA-style grandfathering clause in the RED cybersecurity delegated act itself; check current guidance before assuming otherwise for equipment already in the field.

**What's still pending**

RED's cybersecurity delegated act, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30, is due to be **fully repealed from 11 December 2027** — the same date the CRA reaches full application and its Annex I essential requirements absorb the same ground previously covered by RED Art. 3(3)(d)(e)(f). At that point, references to RED's harmonised cybersecurity standards are also removed from Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/2191, and any assessment of radio equipment's cybersecurity will be carried out exclusively under the CRA — RED itself continues to apply to radio spectrum use and electromagnetic compatibility, just not to cybersecurity. See [`relationship-to-other-eu-law.md`](../relationship-to-other-eu-law.md) and [`../cra/timeline-deadlines.md`](../cra/timeline-deadlines.md) for the full transition timeline.
